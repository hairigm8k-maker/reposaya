Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At HairiHosting, we take your privacy seriously. This page explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can control cookies when using our platform.

1. What We Collect

We only collect the minimum amount of information needed to run HairiHosting:

  • Account info — your email, name, and hashed password
  • Website content — HTML, CSS, JS files you upload
  • Usage analytics — anonymous visit counts for your sites
  • Session data — to keep you logged in securely

2. Cookies We Use

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. We use only what is necessary and helpful:

NamePurposeType
hh_sessionKeeps you logged inEssential
hh_cookie_consentRemembers your cookie choiceEssential
No tracking cookies

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party trackers, or fingerprinting scripts. Your data is not sold to anyone.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Provide hosting services and serve your websites
  • Authenticate your account and secure sessions
  • Send transactional emails (login alerts, password resets, welcome)
  • Improve platform reliability and performance

4. Your Choices

You can control cookies through our banner or your browser settings:

  • Accept — allow all cookies (recommended for full functionality)
  • Reject — disable non-essential cookies (session still required)
  • Browser settings — you can block cookies entirely, but login will not work

5. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data (sites, files, analytics) is permanently removed within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Services

HairiHosting relies on these trusted providers:

  • Cloudflare — CDN, hosting, and security
  • Resend — transactional email delivery

These providers process data only on our behalf and under strict data protection agreements.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at feedback@hairiamri.buzz or via WhatsApp from the dashboard support page.
